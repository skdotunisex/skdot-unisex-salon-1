Frequently asked questions.

Clear answers based on the business information currently supplied for SKDOT.

How do I book?

Use the online appointment request, then send the prepared request to SKDOT on WhatsApp at 09064653935. Appointments are confirmed by SKDOT.

Do you offer home service?

Yes. The supplied price list includes an Exclusive Home Service Call-out at ₦18,000. Availability and arrangements should be confirmed with SKDOT.

Can I see prices before booking?

Yes. Visit the Prices page for the current service prices supplied for the website.

What are the opening hours?

Monday–Tuesday 8:00 AM–9:00 PM; Wednesday–Friday 8:30 AM–9:00 PM; Saturday 9:00 AM–10:00 PM; Sunday 12:00 PM–10:00 PM.

How do I get directions?

Use the official SKDOT Google location link on the Contact page or the Directions button throughout the site.

What if I need a service not listed?

Select “Other / Consultation” in the booking form or contact SKDOT directly on WhatsApp for clarification.
