# SKDOT video library

The website contains the original supplied video collection plus six newly uploaded owner-supplied videos from the latest upload. The six new videos are placed separately on pages where their subject is meaningful, and all six are also available in the Media Library.

New video placements:
- Home — salon welcome
- Services — salon service moment
- About — salon atmosphere
- Gallery — wall/interior detail
- Contact — waiting/arrival area
- Booking — salon floor/styling area

The source videos are vertical 512×910 MP4 files. They are preserved at that native resolution rather than artificially upscaled. Web copies were encoded for faster playback while retaining the original dimensions and no blur/filter effect.
